# Farmer Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 November 2026**

This policy explains how Farmer handles information while providing community and game-support features in Discord. Automatic features may process messages and participation in channels Farmer can access, even when you do not run a command.

## Information collected and used

Farmer processes information needed to provide its features, including:

- Discord user, server, channel, role, message, and interaction IDs, together with relevant usernames, roles, and permissions;
- messages, attachments, and form responses used for support tickets, reports, suggestions, counting, screenshot-channel moderation, staff phrase alerts, and trading archives;
- ticket participants, workflow records, feedback, and staff response metrics;
- XP, levels, participation totals, message identifiers and timestamps used for chat statistics and giveaway checks;
- giveaway entries, winner and claim records, reminders, preferences, marketplace submissions, and moderation-related records; and
- linked Roblox account details and Farming and Friends information requested through Farmer.

Chat participation statistics describe activity recorded through messages and XP awards; they do not measure online status or time connected to Discord. Awarding chat XP does not require reading message text.

Farmer uses this information to operate its features, manage community access, moderate the community, prevent abuse, resolve support requests, and maintain the bot.

## Message content and archived copies

Farmer reads ordinary messages and attachments where required for counting, screenshot-channel enforcement, support transcripts, trading archives, and staff phrase alerts. Matching phrase alerts may include the complete message and a source link in a DM to authorised staff; Farmer checks that the recipient has the required moderation permission and can view the source channel. Giveaway responses may also be forwarded into claim tickets for staff review.

Farmer does not maintain an off-platform archive of every Discord message. Selected content may remain in ticket or trading transcripts, attachment archives, reports, moderation logs, or staff alerts. Deleting an original message or ticket channel does not automatically delete these separate copies.

## Storage and access

Feature records, including Discord identifiers, participation statistics, ticket metadata, giveaway records, preferences, and linked account information, are stored off-platform in MongoDB. Operational logs and exported game logs may also be stored on Farmer's host.

Ticket and trading transcripts and archived ticket attachments are stored in designated Discord channels. Transcript links may use Farmer's viewer at tickets.skeu.link. Access to archive channels is managed through Discord permissions; recipients of transcript or attachment links may be able to share those links.

Information may be processed through Discord, MongoDB, Bloxlink, Roblox, Dunn Games systems, Farmer's hosting services, and authorised community staff as needed for the relevant feature. Farmer does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Information is retained only while needed for Farmer's stated features or where retention is required by law or regulation.

Giveaway message-activity metadata in MongoDB is configured to expire after 31 days. This expiry applies to those activity records, not to all giveaway records or Discord-hosted files. Other records follow their feature-specific lifecycle or are removed when no longer needed. Ticket and trading transcripts and archived attachments do not currently have a fixed automatic expiry period.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, email [terraprivacyrequests@gmail.com](mailto:terraprivacyrequests@gmail.com) and include your Discord user ID. We may request information needed to verify that the records belong to you.

We promptly delete applicable Discord API data upon request, except where retention is required by law or regulation. Requests cover relevant off-platform records and copies maintained by Farmer in Discord. Removing a database record alone does not necessarily remove archived Discord copies.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Farmer's features or data practices change. The date above will show the latest update.

## Contact

Privacy questions and data requests should be sent to [terraprivacyrequests@gmail.com](mailto:terraprivacyrequests@gmail.com).
