# Terra Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 15 August 2026**

This policy explains how Terra handles information when you use the bot.

## Information collected

Terra may process or store:

- Discord user, server, channel, role, message, and interaction IDs;
- usernames, roles, permissions, messages, attachments, and form responses needed for Terra's features;
- tickets and transcripts, reports, suggestions, moderation records, giveaway entries, reminders, levels, and other information you submit; and
- linked Roblox account details and Farming and Friends information requested through Terra.

Terra can read message content for features such as moderation, levelling, counting, automatic responses, and tickets. It does not permanently store every message, although relevant messages may be retained in transcripts, reports, or moderation logs.

## How information is used

Information is used only to operate Terra, provide requested features, moderate the community, prevent abuse, resolve support requests, and maintain the bot.

Information may be processed through Discord, MongoDB, Bloxlink, Roblox, Dunn Games systems, Terra's hosting services, and authorised community staff. Terra does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Information is kept only while it is reasonably needed for Terra's features, moderation, security, or legal requirements. Some records expire automatically; tickets, transcripts, reports, moderation records, and similar records may be kept for longer when still relevant.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact **@Skeusenieth on Discord** and provide your Discord user ID. Some moderation or security records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Terra's features or data practices change. The date above will show the latest update.

## Contact

Privacy questions and data requests may be sent to **@Skeusenieth on Discord**.
